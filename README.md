## Installing

These apps are distributed directly, outside the app stores ([side-loading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sideloading)). Your phone will show extra confirmation prompts — a download warning, an "Allow from this source" toggle, and a Google Play Protect notice. That's expected for any app installed straight from the developer: download the APK on the phone, open it, and tap Install.
