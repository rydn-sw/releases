Rydn Privacy Policy, version 4 (2026-10-21)

Rydn apps are built to keep your information on your phone. We do not run ads, analytics or crash reporting, and we do not sell or share personal information. The only exception is the optional announcement list described below, which our email service provider holds for us. This page says, app by app, what is kept and what leaves your phone.

All apps. Nothing you enter in an app is sent to Rydn unless that app says so below. When you choose to export, share or email something, it goes where you send it (for example your Google Drive or an email you write). Some apps ask rydnsoftware.com whether a newer version exists; that request carries no personal information, but like any web request it reveals your device's IP address to the site's host (GitHub Pages). Every Rydn app allows Android's own backup, so your data can move with you to a new phone: if backup is on in your phone's settings, Android copies the app's data to your Google account. That is Google's service, under Google's policy; you can turn it off in Android's settings.

Level Best. Its only use of the internet is the update check described above; it has no account and sends nothing else. If you turn on Save locations and record a leveling, it keeps the place you type, your remarks, the tilt and, if you allow location, your GPS position, on the phone (and in Android's backup, if it is on). On Android 11 and older the monitor phone asks for location because Android requires it to find Bluetooth devices; Level Best does not use it for anything else. The two phones exchange tilt, a random ID and the phone model over a Bluetooth link that is not paired or encrypted, so a nearby phone could see the ID and read the tilt and phone model.

Voygr. Uses your precise location while it is open or recording a trip, to show speed, heading, elevation and distance. A detailed position log stays on the phone for up to 7 days. Trip totals (no positions) stay until you reset them. Speed-limit databases are downloaded from GitHub without your position.

Rydn Player. Plays music from a folder you choose; files stay where they are. Keeps your star ratings, with file names and paths, on the phone. Reads the names of connected audio devices to pause when they disconnect; it does not keep or send them. Its only network request is the update check described above.

DoStack. Keeps your lists on the phone. If you create or enter a sync key, your lists (item text, list names and times) and a random device ID are sent over an encrypted connection to a server Rydn runs on Oracle Cloud, so your devices can share them. The server records the IP address and time of each connection. It is backed up nightly, encrypted, to Backblaze B2, and backups are kept for up to 90 days. Anyone with your sync key can read that list; keep it private. To have your server data deleted, email support@rydnsoftware.com with your key.

Habits. Keeps your plans, reminders and notes on the phone; reminders are scheduled on the phone. Nothing is sent to Rydn.

Tips. Tips go through Stripe, on Stripe's own page. Rydn sees the amount and the email address you give Stripe, never your card details. Stripe's privacy policy applies.

Websites and web versions. The Rydn websites and web versions set no cookies and run no analytics or advertising. rydnsoftware.com is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, sees the address of the device that visits and may keep it in its logs; the page that offers the downloads also asks GitHub for the list of releases, so GitHub sees that request too. A web version, such as DoStack's, stores what you enter and your settings in your browser's own storage on your device, not in a cookie, and keeps the record that you accepted the license there. It sends your lists to Rydn only if you turn on sync with a key, as the DoStack paragraph above says. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes all of it, including your acceptance. Tips (Stripe) and the optional announcement list (our email service provider) are separate services with their own policies and may set their own cookies on their own pages.

Announcement emails (website only). On rydnsoftware.com you can ask to receive an occasional email about new Rydn apps and releases. If you do, we collect your email address, and we keep the date and time you signed up and the date and time you confirmed. We send you a confirmation email first, and we email you only if you confirm. We use your address only to send these announcements. Our email service provider stores the list and sends the emails for us, under its own privacy policy; it may handle the list in other countries. We do not sell the list or give it to anyone else. Our emails include tracking: our email service provider records, against your address, when you open an email and when you click a link in it, and the links in our emails pass through its address before they reach the page. We use this only to see whether announcements are read. Every email has an unsubscribe link that works in one click, and we stop emailing you when you use it. We keep a record that you unsubscribed so you are not added again by mistake. To ask us to delete your address, including that record, email support@rydnsoftware.com. The Rydn apps never send us your email address for this list, and signing up is not needed to use any app.

Children. Rydn apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact. We will update this page and its version number when anything changes. Questions or requests: support@rydnsoftware.com.
