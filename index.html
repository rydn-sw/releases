Beta for Testing Voygr

Trip computer for the open road, whether you are behind the wheel of a van, an RV, or a big rig. Speed, heading, elevation and trip logs, with speed-limit signs from an on-board database. At 60 miles per hour you travel 88 feet in one second — so Voygr is designed to be glanceable in a fraction of a second, keeping your eyes on the road. A single large button switches between Voygr and Rydn Player (where legal) — no fiddling with Android's recent apps screen.

Latest known: Voygr V30 · published 2026-10-20

Setting it up, and what's new

The first time you open it

  1. On first start Voygr shows the Rydn License and a driving Caution. Read them (Read the full license and Privacy policy open inside the app), then tap I Agree.
  2. Allow Location — While using the app. "Allow all the time" is not needed.
  3. Allow notifications when it asks. While a trip is running Voygr shows a Recording trip notification — that is what lets it keep counting while you are in another app. Refuse it and the notification is hidden, so nothing tells you recording is live.
  4. Put the phone in landscape in its mount. The cockpit layout assumes it.
  5. Tap LOAD on the speed-limit sign, then Download the state or states you drive in. This is a one-time download — after that the speed limits work with no signal at all.

What's new

  • License and caution first: Voygr starts only after you tap I Agree. The license, the privacy policy and the open-source list are inside the app.
  • Keeps counting behind other apps: Voygr's own GPS service keeps a trip recording while you use another app. The speed shows "--" after 3 seconds without a GPS fix.
  • Grade (%) between heading and elevation (it can be turned off in Settings).
  • Export my data: save your trips to the phone, to Google Drive or through the Share menu, and automatically at every Reset and at the end of every drive.
  • Speed limits come only from the onboard OpenStreetMap database; your position never leaves the phone. The "Overpass (Live)" option is gone.
  • App update available in the top row when a newer version is out, an About page with Tell a friend, Share on social media and Support Rydn, and a new icon.

Worth knowing

  • The PLAYER key at the bottom right stays gray until Rydn Player is installed. That is expected, not a fault. To hide it: Setup → Switch to other app(s) button.

Latest known: Rydn Player V22.1 · published 2026-10-18

Setting it up, and what's new

The first time you open it

  1. On first start Rydn Player shows the Rydn License and a driving Caution. Read them, then tap I Agree.
  2. Tap 📂 PICK MUSIC FOLDER and choose your music folder — subfolders are included automatically. Tap Allow / Use this folder when Android asks; the choice is remembered.
  3. To keep your ratings off the phone: ⚙ Settings → Export my data, then Google Drive or a folder on your phone.
  4. Turn the phone to landscape. The deck layout — transport keys, HOLD bar and ratings — is the screen the app is built around.
  5. Optional, under ⚙ Settings: rating filter and weights, Sequential or Shuffle, whether unrated songs play, which folder or database to draw from, and haptics.

What's new

  • On first start Rydn Player shows the Rydn License and a driving Caution. Tap I Agree to begin.
  • Export my data (⚙ Settings, Your data): save your ratings and settings to Google Drive, a folder on your phone, or the Share menu, by hand or automatically once a day. Files go straight into the folder you choose.
  • A yellow App update available button appears when a newer version is out.
  • An About page, with Tell a friend and Support Rydn.
  • The Android Back key steps back through the pages instead of closing Player.
  • A setting for what happens when the audio device disconnects. By default Player moves to another Bluetooth device and keeps playing, and pauses rather than switch to the phone's own speaker.
  • A new icon. The home-screen name is Player.

Worth knowing

  • Back up your ratings: ⚙ Settings → Export my data.
  • VOL + and VOL − move the real Android media volume — the same level the hardware keys move, and a Bluetooth speaker follows it.
  • Installing a new version over an older one keeps your ratings, music folder and settings.
  • Install Voygr too and each app gets a key that jumps straight to the other.

Beta for Testing Level Best

Leveling your rig increases comfort. But if you have an ammonia fridge (propane powered), leveling becomes critical. Each time the fridge is off level, the ammonia overheats. This cumulative damage is dangerous and expensive.

Have an old phone (Android 8+, roughly 2017) gathering dust? Level your rig without climbing in and out of the driver's seat. One phone sits on a fridge shelf as the sensor; a second phone on the dash shows how far off level you are, live, over Bluetooth — no Wi-Fi needed. And you may find a spot that doesn't even require leveling! The first time I used this app, it became my instant favorite.

Latest known: Level Best V33 · published 2026-10-20

Setting it up, and what's new

The first time you open it

  1. On first start Level Best shows the Rydn License and a driving Caution. Read them, then tap I Agree.
  2. You need two Android phones (Android 8 or newer) with Bluetooth, and Level Best installed on both — the same app does both jobs. No Wi-Fi and no account. Its only internet use is a small check at rydnsoftware.com for a newer version; it sends nothing about you.
  3. Open Level Best on the first phone. Tap SENSOR, allow Nearby devices and notifications. Place the sensor on the fridge shelf or other preferred location.
  4. Open Level Best on the second phone. Tap MONITOR, put the phone in your dash mount or other preferred location. Carefully move your rig and monitor.

What's new

  • License and caution first: Level Best starts only after you tap I Agree. The license and the privacy policy are inside the app.
  • An About page with feedback, Tell a friend with a QR code, Share on social media and Support Rydn.
  • A yellow App update available button on both phones when a newer version is out.
  • Exit now stays closed.
  • The sideways layout fits, with the role line and buttons.

Worth knowing

  • Known issue: if Android's backup is restored onto a second phone, it copies this app's settings, including the sensor ID, zero offsets and link, so two phones can end up with the same sensor ID. A fix is planned for V33.1.
  • Each phone explains why it needs Bluetooth before Android asks. The sensor asks for Nearby devices and Notifications; the monitor asks for Nearby devices only.
  • If two Level Best sensors are within Bluetooth range, a monitor connects to the first one it hears.
  • Questions: support@rydnsoftware.com

Beta for Testing DoStack

To-do lists that sync between your phone and any browser. Works offline and catches up later; no VPN needed.

⬇ INSTALL DoStack v30

Latest known: DoStack v30 · published 2026-10-21

Or use it in a browser — nothing to install: https://dostack.rydnsoftware.com
The address is the same for everyone. Paste your key in the ⚙ gear menu to see your own list.

Getting your key. You only need one if you want the same list on more than one device. Open DoStack, tap the ⚙ gear, then tap START A NEW LIST. The app makes your key and shows it once: save it somewhere, because it is not shown again. To use the same list on another phone or in a browser, paste the key into that device's ⚙ gear menu and press SET. The gear menu's OPEN ON ANY DEVICE has the web address and a COPY KEY button.

About syncing. Sync is optional — without a key, DoStack stays on that one device. With a key, your lists are stored on a small server in Oracle Cloud so your phone and browser stay in step. The connection is encrypted, and your key is what keeps other users out of your list. Your lists are not encrypted against us: we run the server and could in theory read what is on it, so treat DoStack as a to-do list, not a place for passwords or private personal details.

Setting it up, and what's new

The first time you open it

  1. Start using it straight away — type in the bar at the top and the item is saved. Installing does nothing on its own.
  2. To see the same list on another phone or browser you need a key. Either paste one you were given into the ⚙ gear menu and press SET, or open the ⚙ gear menu and tap START A NEW LIST to make one on the phone.
  3. A new key is shown once. Save it somewhere — it is not shown again.
  4. No key? It still works as a private list on that device and says SYNC has no KEY. That is a normal state, not an error.

What's new

  • START A NEW LIST in the gear menu makes your own key on the phone, so you no longer have to be given one.
  • The web address is inside the app now: ⚙ gear → OPEN ON ANY DEVICE, with the address as a link plus COPY and COPY KEY. Copying can fail inside the phone app; when it does the app says so, and you can select the text by hand.
  • Archive (⚙ gear) keeps everything you finish or delete, with the dates it was added and closed, and a RESTORE button. Export archive saves the whole history as a spreadsheet. On the phone that opens the share sheet, so you can send it to Drive, Gmail or Files.
  • Deleting a stack asks first, and its items move to Main, so nothing is lost.
  • Each item now shows only its checkbox, its text and the X, so the list reads cleanly. The Colors and Move buttons appear whenever Settings is open — or all the time, if you turn on COLORS & MOVE on items in Settings. That switch starts off and is set per device. Move is how you send an item to another stack.
  • A newly added item no longer opens its color picker. To have it open, turn on COLOR on new items in Settings; it starts off and is set per device.
  • Items are more compact, so more of your list fits on screen and long items wrap later.

Worth knowing

  • Everything works offline. Sync catches up by itself when the network comes back.
  • Treat your key like a password. It stays in whatever phone or browser you paste it into, so don't paste it on a shared or public computer.
  • Upgrading: just install over the old version. Your items, your key and your settings are kept, and there is nothing to change in settings afterward.
